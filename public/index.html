      Tips to raise your score

      • Match the JD's exact wording — "project management" scores better than "managed projects" against a keyword-matching ATS.
      • Re-run the check per job. The "important" keywords change with every posting, so a resume tuned for one JD won't automatically score well on another.
      • Don't add a missing keyword you can't back up — a skill that doesn't hold up in an interview costs more than a slightly lower match score.

      Frequently asked questions

      What is an ATS (Applicant Tracking System)?

      An ATS is software companies use to collect, filter, and rank job applications before a recruiter ever opens them. Many ATS platforms score or filter resumes based on how well they match the keywords in the job posting — this tool gives you that same kind of keyword view of your own resume.

      Is this resume checker really free?

      Yes. You get 5 free checks per day, no account or credit card required. The keyword matching runs in your browser, so there's no per-check cost to offer it for free.

      Does this tool store or upload my resume?

      No. Your resume and job description are processed entirely in your browser and never uploaded, stored, or logged. The one exception is the optional "Get AI verdict" button, which sends that text to Google's Gemini API only when you click it.

      How accurate is the match score?

      It's a directional signal, not a guarantee. Real ATS software varies widely in how it parses and scores resumes. This tool highlights the keywords a typical ATS is likely to look for so you can close obvious gaps — it isn't a simulation of any specific company's system.

      Can I use this for every job I apply to?

      Yes — re-run the check for each job description, since the important keywords change with every posting. You get 5 free checks per day, which resets at midnight.
